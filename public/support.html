💡 Common Troubleshooting Guides

1. Push Notification Issues

Ensure notifications are enabled on your device. On iOS standalone apps, go to your iPhone Settings -> Safari -> Page Settings and verify that "Notifications" are set to Allow. Inside the app, click the Bell icon in the header to resubscribe.

2. Offline Mode Syncing

If you are operating in a remote area with poor cell coverage, all scores and deductions remain saved locally in the browser/app cache. Do not clear your browser history or app storage. The data will upload automatically once your device reconnects to a cellular or Wi-Fi network.

3. Live Audio/Walkie-Talkie Failures

Our Walkie-Talkie requires microphone access permission. If you block microphone access, WebRTC calls will fail. Go to your browser or system permissions and ensure microphone access is granted for the app.
