How to Use VBT App

Interactive Flowcharts & Feature Walkthroughs

🔐 Flow 1: Portal Login & Authentication

The platform requires identification to allocate correct permissions. Here is the lifecycle of logging into the portal:

Step 1
Boot App
Legitimate sessions sign in anonymously automatically on boot.
Step 2
Select Portal Role
Click Volunteer, Game Leader, Servant, or Coordinator.
Step 3
Passcode / Name
Input credentials or match your name on the roster.

Once complete, the client unlocks matching tabs and settings. Volunteers get instantly redirected to the dashboard without entering passcodes. Referees/Game Leaders will automatically have their assigned station selected.

Game Leader Flow 2: Live Scoring & Match Completions

Referees are in charge of reporting results on the field. Follow this visual pipeline to record scores:

Step 1
Find Assigned Game
Your assigned game is auto-selected. You can manage matchups under the Schedule tab.
Step 2
Adjust Live Score
Tap point increments (+/-) to record points for Shakes/Fries.
Step 3
Toggle Match Complete
Turn on the "Complete Match" switch to finalize results.
Step 4
Recalculate Scoreboard
Scoreboard updates automatically, shifting the Tug-of-War balance!

Team Leader Flow 3: Submitting Point Deductions

Servants can log point deductions for rules infractions. Here is the operational loop:

Step 1
Open Schedule Tab
View your customized team timeline (e.g. Red 1).
Step 2
Click "Deduct Points"
Open the point deduction modal form.
Step 3
Select Infraction
Choose a preset infraction or type a custom reason. Click submit.

🖥️ Flow 4: Desktop Split-Grid Workspace

When launching the portal on large screens (desktops or laptops), the app automatically unlocks a split screen:

Step 1
Access Schedule / Scores
Open either Schedule or Scoreboard tab on a desktop screen.
Step 2
Split-Grid Activation
The app displays the Timeline on the left and the Scoreboard/Tug-of-War on the right.
Step 3
Simultaneous Control
Coordinators can manage schedule pings while watching scores live on the same screen!

💾 Flow 5: Coordinator Settings & Individual Section Saving

Coordinators can update configurations page-by-page. To prevent accidental data overrides, each section has a dedicated save button:

Step 1
Modify Data Section
Edit Team Names, Station/Game Names, or Servant Assignments in their inputs.
Step 2
Click "Save Section"
Tap the individual save button located inside the edited section container.
Step 3
Success Toast
A green success notification slides in, confirming updates have been committed to the database.

🎮 Complete Station & Game Rules Manual

Below is the official reference manual for all 6 games played at VBT Sports Camp, including rules, equipment needed, and assigned leaders:

Game 1: Blind Builder Leaders: Micho, Emily

Rules: One player is blindfolded and is given plastic cups. They must build a pyramid through verbal instructions from his/her teammates or a single teammate. Each teammate stays stacking until he/she stacks a full pyramid, then the next team member takes his/her turn.

Scoring: The team with the highest number of stacks completed wins.

Equipment Needed:

  • 2 Blindfolds
  • 20 Plastic Cups
  • 2 Tables
  • 10 Tokens
Game 2: Skee Ball Leaders: Macarious, Dani

Rules: Each team member gets a try at throwing the ball and dropping it in the holes. Referees record accumulated scores.

Scoring: Team with the highest accumulated score wins.

Reference: Classic Skee-Ball - Fodor Billiards

Equipment Needed:

  • Skee ball
  • Tokens
  • Table
Game 3: Minefield Leaders: Nathalie, Kiro

Rules: One player from each team is blindfolded and must cross from one point to the other with obstacles placed throughout. One team member is chosen to guide him or the whole team does. At the end of the obstacle course, the blindfolded team member places a cone with his team's color in an XO (Tic-Tac-Toe) game.

Scoring: The team with the most XO wins receives one token each.

Equipment Needed:

  • 2 Blindfolds
  • Cones (at least 3 of each color)
  • Obstacles
  • Tokens
  • 4 pieces of rope
Game 4: Helium Stick & Human Chairs Leaders: Karim, John, Cinderella, Patrick

Rules: Each team is split into two halves: one team of four (boys only or girls only) for the Human Chair game, and two teams of three for the Helium Stick game.

  • Helium Stick: Each team member must put two fingers under the broomstick and they must lower it to the ground together without losing contact.
  • Human Chairs Circle: Team members build a circle by lying on each other's laps. Chairs are placed under them initially, then removed one by one until they support themselves.

Equipment Needed:

  • 4 Broom sticks
  • 8 Chairs
Game 5: Whiffle Ball Leaders: Joice, Jessica

Rules: Each team member is given a colored ball and must throw it in its corresponding colored hole. Each team member gets one try.

Scoring: Team with the most goals wins.

Equipment Needed:

  • Whiffle Ball
  • Table
  • Tokens
Game 6: Blind Shape Leaders: Bassem, Sara

Rules: Each team is given a rope and are blindfolded. They are told to create a specific shape (square, triangle, etc.) with the rope. The referee reviews correctness.

Scoring: Team with the highest amount of correct shapes wins.

Equipment Needed:

  • 2 Long ropes
  • 20 Blindfolds
